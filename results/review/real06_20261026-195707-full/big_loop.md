# The big loop: the house score per cycle

Each cycle: the AI judge scores every final render 1-5 as a client would (the problems it names are the next round's work, with the code's and the inspector's), the agent fixes, the changed views are rendered again and judged again; a cycle that lowers the score is rolled back, the loop goes on while the score rises (at least 0.5) and time is left. Score = 60% the judge's mean (1-5 as 0-100) + 40% defects (100 / (1 + points per view / 5): 10 points per critical, 3 per major finding).
